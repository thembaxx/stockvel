# Brand exploration · 14 October 2026

The product needs a recognisable community-administration identity for rotating stokvels and burial societies. The attached app-icon reference informs distinctive silhouettes, rounded tiles and restrained depth. No icon in that reference is copied.

## Brand foundation

- Promise: a clear record of contributions, evidence and shared decisions.
- Audience: South African treasurers, secretaries, committees and their members.
- Voice: warm, direct and precise. Say “reported” and “verified” explicitly; avoid bank, wallet, insurance or guaranteed-benefit language.
- Core palette: navy `#293B70`, powder blue `#C4E7FB`, cool paper `#F7F9FC`, ink `#263451`. Green and amber communicate workflow status rather than dominate the brand.
- Typography: Inter for the wordmark, interface and tabular financial figures; Instrument Serif italic for a few editorial headlines.
- Photography: everyday South African community life, candid smartphone use, natural daylight, cool blue clothing and warm skin tones. Generated imagery is illustrative, never a customer testimonial.
- Logo requirements: custom silhouette, readable at 16–32px, flat monochrome alternative, accessible light/dark lockups, and safe margins within a maskable app icon. Gradients add depth to the app tile; the symbol must work without them.
- Motion: quiet entrances and short tactile feedback. Respect reduced motion; financial amounts remain stable.

## Naming candidates for visual review

1. **StokvelOS** — retains continuity and category clarity. It sounds technical and is already used by another community-finance product; keep it as a working name only while comparing alternatives.
2. **Stokuni** — a coined blend of “stokvel” and “unity”, not a claimed translation. Short, connected to the category and suitable for a shared-circle symbol. Pair it with “Stokvel & burial society administration”.
3. **OurStok** — combines shared ownership with an accessible category cue. A warmer, conversational direction that can use a linked-loop monogram. Pair it with the same category descriptor to avoid ambiguity.

Suggested line: “Good things grow together.” More functional supporting line: “Community money, made clear.”

This is preliminary screening only, not trademark, company-name, domain or app-store clearance. No domain has been bought or claimed. Native-speaker review is required before attaching a South African language meaning to any invented name.

No replacement name or logo has been rolled into the app yet. The visual concepts are for choosing the identity before generating final SVG, monochrome, favicon, PWA and social assets.
